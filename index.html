About

Behind the mic.

AC Cotton is a voice actor whose work runs from national brand campaigns through to long-form documentary narration. The through-line is trust: a read that sounds like a person talking, not a script being performed.

Trained in theatre and shaped by years of studio work, AC brings an actor's instinct for intention to every session — finding the reason a line exists before finding the sound of it.

Clients come back for the turnaround as much as the read. Files land edited, mastered, and matched to spec, ready to drop straight into the timeline.
